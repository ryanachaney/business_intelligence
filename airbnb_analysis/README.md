# Airbnb Market Analysis Findings

1. High-volume hosts (10+ listings) account for 31.9% of all listings in Chicago, representing significant market concentration.
2. In Columbus, the North Linden neighborhood offers the lowest median price per person at $32.60 among qualifying areas.
3. Entire homes in the Twin Cities generate a median estimated annual revenue of $19,619.
4. Revenue concentration is highest in Chicago, where the top 10% of listings earn 38.3% of total market revenue.
5. Twin Cities Superhosts experience a -$1 median nightly price premium ($267 vs $268), indicating the badge does not yield price power in that market.
